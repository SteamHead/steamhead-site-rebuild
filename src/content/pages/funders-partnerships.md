hero_tagline: SteamHead is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit that runs on grants, donations, and volunteer time — here's how to partner with us or give directly.
intro_body: >
  We don't run a menu of sponsorship tiers with fixed minimums. Most funding
  and partnership conversations start with an invitation: we visit an
  education community over a summer or winter break, and while we're there we
  discuss whether a longer-term program would be valuable for that
  community. Funding requests are handled project by project, shaped around
  what each community actually needs.


  We're happy to meet with potential funders and partners and find ways to
  work together that match our capacity as a small, volunteer-run
  organization — there's no fixed minimum grant or donation size for a formal
  partnership. Reach out and we'll have a conversation.
get_involved_body: >
  **Donate.** Donations of any amount are welcome and go directly toward our
  mission of making hands-on, project-based STEAM education more accessible.
  As a registered 501(c)(3), donations to SteamHead are tax-deductible to the
  extent allowed by law — see our [Nonprofit Status](/form-990-n/) page to
  verify our standing directly with the IRS.


  **Fund a program.** If you'd like to support a specific program —
  MakeFashion Edu, Neighborhood Earth, System Upgrade, or a school
  partnership like ours with SFUSD — tell us about it and we'll talk through
  what's realistic.


  **Invite us.** SteamHead goes into communities where it's invited. If
  you're an educator, school, or community organization curious whether a
  SteamHead program would fit, that's exactly the kind of conversation we
  want to have.
