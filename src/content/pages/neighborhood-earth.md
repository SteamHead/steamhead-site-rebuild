Neighborhood Earth is SteamHead's longest running program — existing before SteamHead itself. In 2011, James entered China as a manufacturing engineer, found that locals highly valued education, and started free classes for factory workers.

Since then, James and Carrie have held free classes in Brazil, Germany, Canada, China, the UK, and the US.
