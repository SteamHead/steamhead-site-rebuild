**What is advocacy?**

The dictionary defines advocacy as "public support for or recommendation of a particular cause or policy."

Fashion has been used to advocate for (or against) a cause throughout history. With the inclusion of technology, we can tell our advocacy story even more clearly. Let's look at how this has been done in the past.

📄 **[Fashion as Activism](/downloads/mfedu-course/MFEdu_Fashion_as_Activism.pdf)** (PDF Presentation)

Historical examples covered in the presentation:
- **Women's Suffrage** — use of white, purple, and green
- **Zoot Suits** — symbol of cultural identity and resistance
- **Black Panthers**
- **Hong Kong Umbrella Movement**
- **LGBTQ+ Pride**
- **Thailand's Red Shirts**
- **Guy Fawkes**
