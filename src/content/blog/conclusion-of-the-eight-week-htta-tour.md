The HTTA: Hackers Tour Through Asia, started on the 12th of March just before the Singapore Foss Asia 2019 Summit and concluded in Shenzhen on the 1st of June 2019 after nearly three months of traveling by bus, train and plane across the huge continent. A big shout-out to SteamHead for the fascinating three week Residency at their Maker Space. What a way to wind up the tour. While in Shenzhen we managed to fit in tours of Shenzhen based Maker Schools, Maker Spaces, a 3D Printing R & D Lab, factories, the Shenzhen Laser Faire and Smart Home Trade Shows and of course many pleasant hours were spent in the HQB electronics market area.

You can read and see more about the HTTA tour here: [Instagram](http://www.instagram.com/hackerstriptoasia/)
