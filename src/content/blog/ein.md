What is EIN? It's our monthly Educators Meetup! SteamHead supports EIN as its Community Partner.

EIN is composed of:

- Public Schools (Chinese public schools)
- Private Schools (International schools)
- Community Partners (After school educators)

Every month we have a translated English/Chinese meeting at a school, where that school features its educational innovations and we have a series of short presentations that teachers can use in their classrooms.
