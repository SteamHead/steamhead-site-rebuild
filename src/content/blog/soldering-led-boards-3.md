Paul was busy working on a personal project at SteamHead one morning when James called up saying that he would be over with a gentleman called Fisherman to visit the space. He presumed it was to show him around the space and explain what happens at SteamHead.

It turns out it was to solder on some LED PH connectors onto some LED breakout boards. He loves to solder, so he jumped in and helped them out. Qiu who was also a visiting resident had never soldered before, so he gave her a quick tutorial on how to solder and her board came out really well.
