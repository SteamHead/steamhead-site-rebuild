## Dream, Dream, Dream

Designed and Modeled by: Camila Nolasco 9 yrs, Victoria Espinoza 9 yrs, Polett Gutierrez 9 yrs, Victoria Ortiz 8 yrs

Sometimes we dream at night and sometimes we daydream. But we all have big dreams. Our dreams of the future include becoming a veterinarian, a wild animal doctor, a fashion business woman and a people doctor. But our dreams of the present are of pandas, puppies, bunnies and teddy bears, small dreams which might lead to the bigger ones. We decided to make nightgowns and to use lights and shadows to make our dream subject. Our animals disappear when the lights are off and show up when our lights are on.

Cami- I dream of taking care of wild animals such as pandas, which is why I chose a panda to carry. Besides that I love pandas because they are so cute!

Victoria E- I dream of becoming a veterinarian and my shadow puppy represents this. Either a vet …or a dentist.

Polett-I dream of becoming a fashionista. But not just telling people what to wear, I want to make and sell my own fashions. I’m thinking about using a bunny as part of my logo!

Victoria O- I dream of becoming a doctor. I want to take care of people and that is why I chose a Teddy Bear. Teddy Bears have comforted people for a long time and I want people to be happy and healthy.

**Materials and Tech- Satin/Taffeta, elastic, quilt batting, upholstery, fairy lights, flexi-bits.**
