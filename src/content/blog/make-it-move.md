Land, Sea, and Air! In Make it Move you will be challenged to create working toy cars, boats, and quadcopters! Learn to build vehicles that can move through anything in this 16 session course!

Each student will build 3 vehicles, and take them home at the end of the course. We will also build training and obstacle courses for each of the vehicles!
