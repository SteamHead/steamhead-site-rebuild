The Great Things Blog is where we share what's happening across SteamHead's programs and communities.

Here you'll find student project documentation, educator reflections, event recaps, and ideas from the global network of makers and teachers we work with.

## What to Expect

- **Project stories** — the process behind student work, not just the finished product
- **Educator perspectives** — teachers sharing what worked, what didn't, and what they learned
- **Event recaps** — runway shows, maker fairs, workshops, and community gatherings
- **Resources and ideas** — tools, lesson structures, and approaches worth sharing

We believe that learning is strongest when it's documented and shared. This blog is part of that practice.
