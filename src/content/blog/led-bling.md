Tech Out and Gear Up, we are adding LEDs and electronics to everything wearable!

- LED Glasses, Shirts? check
- LED crowns, swords, shoes? you can craft it!
- Big Cardboard Robots, Crowns of Flowers? You can bring any materials you want to the class, to modify and add LED Bling!
- Sailor moon costumes, Robot ninja costumes? Try it! Start it and see how cool you can make it!

![A skirt lit with dozens of tiny LEDs](/images/2017/10/led-bling-skirt.png)

## What's next?

These 8 sessions are your introduction to costume design. Cosplay, movie props, Halloween creations? Begin your journey with LED Bling!

**Make Fashion, Shenzhen School Exhibit:** In Spring 2018 we will attend a Make Fashion exhibit for students! All students from this class can apply for acceptance to show off their BLING!

![A person in a full cardboard robot costume](/images/2017/10/led-bling-cardboard-robot.jpg)

**Activities:** Costume design, drawing, cutting, sewing, gluing, molding. Electronics, wiring, lighting.

**Requirements:** Age 7+

**Materials:** Class provides LEDs, costume foam, fabrics, cardboard.

**Optional Materials:** Students can bring in their own clothes, glasses, or other materials to modify.

![A child in a silver robot costume](/images/2017/10/led-bling-robot-costume.jpg)

## Parents!

Interested but can't make it? For an intro to creating Wearable Electronics with your child at home, contact us and we will send you some great websites to get you started!
