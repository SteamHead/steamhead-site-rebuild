The Royal Donuts, Team makerSAIS, 2018 Shenzhen MakeFashion EduThrough the study of texture, students took inspiration from American artist Wayne Thiebaud. His textured paintings that focused on cakes and desserts spawned the idea for this team’s creation. It was finally Disney’s Cookie Carnival Symphony that helped the young designers solidify their idea of the Royal Donut Court with Donut Queen and Donut King. The effort has involved a team of mixed ages – from grade 1 to 8, and their collaborative efforts have proved fruitful as they worked through concept creation, design, revision, and final works.
