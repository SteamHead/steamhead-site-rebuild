## Maker Ed Empower (Non Profit)

At SteamHead we want to see Maker Education brought into classrooms everywhere. We provide free resources to teachers and schools looking to manage their own programs.

- **Maker Badges** — a system to organize and formalize classroom Maker Ed experiences.
- **Equipment List** — recommended makerspace tools and supplies from local Chinese suppliers.
- **Maker Ed Meetup** — weekly events for teachers to share and learn.
- **Sponsor of: EIN Meetup** — monthly school events for teachers to tour local schools and receive presentations on that school's education innovations.

## PBL Connect

PBL Connect is our program that brings together education providers with communities. Starting Maker Ed classes, professional development at schools, and Project Based Learning consulting for schools looking to develop full programs.
