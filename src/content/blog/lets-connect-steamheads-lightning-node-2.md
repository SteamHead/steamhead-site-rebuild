SteamHead is actively researching and building interactive curriculum about financial forces and how it impacts us on an individual and community level - [SteamBeans](/beans/). It is a work in progress, please check back periodically or sign up for our newsletter by scrolling all the way down to the footer on the bottom right. A part of this research involves our exploration into cutting-edge technology like the [Lightning Network](https://www.investopedia.com/terms/l/lightning-network.asp). Most students will simply use this node to transact, but some students will go on to create their own nodes, this will be the first node they open a channel with!
