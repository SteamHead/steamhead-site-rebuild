At theMake.club we train young engineers in skills and values from Silicon Valley's [Maker Movement](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maker_culture). The Maker Movement grew in California out of the DIY culture, focusing on technology, sharing, and learning-by-doing. Members are guided through engineering projects, not only creating exciting products but also practicing methods of creativity, planning, and presentation.

Barack Obama and Xi Jinping are both supporting Makers with government funding, and leaders worldwide recognize the value of high-tech education and innovation. At theMake.club we are building a bridge between Chinese and USA Makers by introducing kids from both countries to each other, enabling them to share projects and experiences.

Founders Ben Simpson & Carrie Leung have worked in high tech companies in San Francisco, they are now in Shenzhen enjoying the opportunity to explore the world. By sharing their love of Making, students will have an opportunity to practice interesting and useful skills, and find their own love of turning ideas into reality.

## About Us

From San Francisco, to China. We come from Fortune 500 companies in Silicon Valley, now here in Shenzhen sharing experiences with young engineers. Children working with us learn DIY hi-tech innovation, pitching ideas in English, and working with multinational teams.

Our members create real products, present them to engineers as an entrepreneur would, and bring their projects on the road to Maker Faires in Shenzhen and Silicon Valley.
