Friendship, Team Moralture, Shezhen MakeFashion Edu 2018 Composed of Athena Hu and Molly Li, eight years old, the Friendship Dress has been inspired by the friendship the two designers have shared for five years now. Their piece incorporates LED lighting and fabric artwork on a dress format. Describing the design process as a challenge in the project, an earlier comment by Athena sheds light on their final piece: “Molly and I \[have been\] friends since we were three years old. We love to play princess games. One day, under the starry night, we played \[our\] favorite princess game again. Something felt special. That is ‘friendship’.”
