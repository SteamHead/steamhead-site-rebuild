During the three week SteamHead residency, Paul spent many happy working hours on some projects in the SteamHead main work room. While most of the time was spent designing up and 3D printing a windup butterfly release mechanism, along with a corresponding hand held winding tool, there was ample time to have a play with some of the Waveshare e-Paper products and colourful LED strips.
