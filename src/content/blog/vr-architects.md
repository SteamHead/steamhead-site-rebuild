Make your own VR model of real buildings! Each student will create a 3D building model that can be walked through with VR headsets, and shared to anyone with a smartphone.

![A laptop, smartphone and cardboard VR headset](/images/2017/10/vr-architects-kit.png)

## Why Build in VR?

Design a $1 million bedroom for yourself! Design a school for students with superpowers! Add a fashion catwalk to your classroom! Build cool stuff, and make it feel real.

## What's next?

After this 8 project session, join our Games Workshop and bring your Virtual Building with you! Build a game inside your own VR world!

**Shenzhen Dream Builders Exhibit:** One building model from a student in Shenzhen will be selected to host a VR Maker Exhibition, hold other students projects, and shared with students in the US!

![Students in a classroom, one wearing a VR headset](/images/2017/10/vr-architects-classroom.jpg)

**Activities:** 3D Modeling, creating floorplans, pencil drafting, VR programming

**Requirements:** Laptop, smart phone, age 7+

![A virtual gallery seen in split-screen VR view](/images/2017/10/vr-architects-gallery.png)

## Parents!

Interested but can't make it? For an intro to using VR at home with your child, contact us and we will send you the CoSpaces parent tour.
