## FACILITATE THE CURRICULUM

SteamHead offers a MakeFashion Edu online course. During these courses, educators will participate in experiences that can be used with students to explore storytelling through fashion tech as well as the development of skills.

The process can guide instructors through a learning sequence or they can select pieces which fit within their own program. Essential activity sheets, slides, and videos are readily available to download.
