## run the program

## PUBLISH WITH MAKEFASHION EDU

MakeFashion Edu would love to introduce your student projects to our community through our social media and education network channels.

Contact **[james@steamhead.space](mailto:james@steamhead.space)** to arrange for free publishing options and for your student work to be featured on official MakeFashion Edu channels!

Additionally, we love when the entire community including parents shares pictures of your event and projects on social media using the hashtag **#MakeFashionEdu**

We welcome you to view the works of past students!
