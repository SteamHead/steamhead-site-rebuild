MakeFashion Edu's 2019 Calgary runway was part of Glow: Downtown Winter Light Festival, an annual family-friendly light and music festival in the heart of downtown Calgary. Calgary is the home of MakeFashion, the professional designer platform behind MakeFashion Edu, so students here were mentored by professional designers who work with technology on the runway.

Read more: [MakeFashion Edu at Glow - Spectrum](/blog/makefashion-edu-at-glow-spectrum/).
