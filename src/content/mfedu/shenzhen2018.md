The first MakeFashion Edu STEAM Runway. Students from Shenzhen schools and after-school groups, including Shenzhen American International School (SAIS) and Moralture Education Group, entered the STEAM Runway challenge and won spots on the runway. Electronics company owners and technology entrepreneurs joined teachers in mentoring the students.

Read more about the projects: [Guitar of Light](/blog/guitaroflight/), [Razor Wings](/blog/razor-wings/) and [Friendship](/blog/friendship-team-moralture-shezhen-makefashion-edu-2018/).
