High Tech Middle is a project-based learning school whose approach has influenced schools around the world. San Marcos was the first MakeFashion Edu program where projects drew on Humanities, Science and Design classes together. That collaboration between teachers gave students cross-disciplinary experience and produced projects with deep advocacy and social purposes.
