Shenzhen's second MakeFashion Edu runway, and the program's fourth show since launch. Returning students brought deeper stories and more advanced tech, and new students joined with dozens of earlier projects to inspire them. The team also filmed more of the show and produced open-source teaching videos from it.

Read more: [Shenzhen 2019 Runway Full Version](/blog/shenzhen-2019-runway-full/) and [Shenzhen 2019 Highlights](/blog/shenzhen-2019-runway-highlights-makefashion-edu/).
