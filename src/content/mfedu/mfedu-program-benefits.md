## PROGRAM BENEFITS

At MakeFashion Edu our goal is to guide educators, mentors, makerspace facilitators, after-school leaders, or anyone interested, in a process that allows young designers to develop skills and ideas to tell their stories through fashion tech pieces they create and share. The MakeFashion Edu Process will expand the possibilities for creation and innovation because the students will have the tools and materials to move ideas from their minds to the physical world.
