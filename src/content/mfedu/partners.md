Every organization on this page has supported and taken part in MakeFashion
Edu — sponsoring runway shows, hosting classes and workshops, contributing
hardware and materials, or bringing the program to their students. Programs
like these happen because schools, companies, and communities show up for
student makers, and we're proud to call these organizations our friends.
