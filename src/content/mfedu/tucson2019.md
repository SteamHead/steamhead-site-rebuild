Tucson's first MakeFashion Edu runway. Students from three Tucson Unified School District (TUSD) schools spent three months designing their own fashion tech before walking the runway. Their teachers, three veterans of the project-based learning program at Shenzhen American International School, brought MakeFashion Edu back to the US with them.

Read more: [Tucson MakeFashion Edu](/blog/tucson-makefashion-edu/) and the [behind-the-scenes documentary](/blog/mfedu-az-docu/).
