MakeFashion Edu is an opportunity for students to express themselves through fashion tech. Students work in teams to design, construct, and present their e-wearables to a public audience. The process is highlighted during the exhibition and each team has a story to tell. These items will expand the possibilities for creation and innovation because the students will have the tools and materials to move ideas from their minds to the physical world.

Educators can review our [course guides on The Shelf](/theshelf/) to get an idea of what resources (materials, tools, space and expertise) are needed before launching your class.

Use this three-month calendar of activities as a guide for all the events leading up to the runway show. Note that this is just for reference and you can tailor your calendar to suit your runway show!
