Anina is an international model turned maker! She joined us in Shenzhen to bring attitude and fashion to electronics and education. Find her on [http://www.anina.net/](http://www.anina.net/) and [http://www.360fashion.net/](http://www.360fashion.net/)

Anina ran several ed workshops in the community, and also presented to the local maker community for entrepreneurial advice:  

  
-   E-Wear Workshop (SAIS Schools)
  
-   [Kombucha Bio-Leather Fashion](https://makezine.com/2018/03/01/making-faux-leather-from-kombucha/) (SteamHead)
  
-   LED Bling Hackathon (Harbor School)
  
-   Hardware Massive Talk
  
-   Hi Tech | Hi Fashion, Women’s Maker Workshop (SteamHead)
  

  
The Hardware Massive talk was particularly successful in bringing together the women’s maker workshop!

Overall, we directly impacted over 30 students, 20 teachers (wow!), and 100 makers in Shenzhen.
