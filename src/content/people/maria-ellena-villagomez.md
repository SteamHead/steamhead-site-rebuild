Maria Ellena Villagomez brings a dynamic blend of creative arts, design, and community-centred innovation to SteamHead’s board. As a graphic designer and co-founder of MakeFashion, Maria has championed the fusion of fashion, technology, and hands-on maker culture—helping youth and emerging creators explore STEM through expressive wearable tech. She has also been deeply involved with FUSE33 Makerspace, contributing to programs that support collaborative making, learning, and creative entrepreneurship. Maria’s work spans international creative communities and reflects a passion for building inclusive spaces where art, technology, and education intersect.
