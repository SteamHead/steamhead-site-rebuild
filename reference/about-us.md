## About Us

SteamHead is a network of people, spaces, and events who collaborate to improve equity in education as the path forward.

We believe that by embracing design thinking and a maker-mindset, education can be more engaging and meaningful, and with that more effective in preparing students for success.

## Our Mission

To provide access to quality, relevant education focused on integrating design, technology, and community.

## Our VISION

We envision a world where individuals are confident in their abilities, free to question, and have access to the resources and opportunities they need to build a fulfilling life of their own definition.

## Our Values

We are committed to making education easy to understand and available to everyone. Our focus is on ensuring that learning is accessible to all, regardless of background or circumstances.

Our goal is not just to teach subjects but to build confidence in students. We want them to feel sure about themselves, their abilities, and their potential to succeed in their studies and beyond.

We believe in connecting what students learn with the bigger picture of the human story. Through Science, Technology, Engineering, Arts, and Mathematics (STEAM), we aim to help students see how their education fits into the larger narrative of human progress and innovation.

## We're not a company that only does education but rather an umbrella for our educators to focus around.

### SteamHead is a nonprofit founded by educators with a goal to openly share educational resources and teaching ideas.

This initiative brings teachers from different backgrounds together on a platform that not only encourages but celebrates the sharing of ideas and learning materials. The organization believes that when educators worldwide collaborate, it can significantly improve teaching methods and educational experiences.

### In the SteamHead community, teachers can freely exchange teaching materials, strategies, and perspectives.

This open sharing is seen as crucial for creating a more enriched educational environment, allowing teachers to learn from each other's successes and challenges. Through a culture of openness and teamwork, SteamHead aims to empower educators by providing a supportive network that enhances the quality of education for students worldwide.

## Funding

SteamHead is proud to be self-funded, which means we run independently and don't rely on outside sources for money. This independence gives us the freedom to focus on our goals without being influenced by external factors. We're committed to staying true to our mission and maintaining the freedom to pursue our initiatives.

Additionally, we work together with schools to secure grants. This collaboration involves actively participating in the grant writing process, tailoring our efforts to meet the specific needs of each school or community. By doing this, we support schools and communities effectively, making a positive impact on educational opportunities and the learning experiences of students.
