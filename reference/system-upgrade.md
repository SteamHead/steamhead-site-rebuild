# System Upgrade

Empowering educators with innovative tools and resources to redefine learning and create impactful, future-ready education for a changing world.

The System Upgrade initiative equips educators with tools and resources to revolutionize education, disrupt outdated standards, and deliver impactful, relevant content across diverse fields.

## STEAMHEAD & THE SFUSD

We are thrilled to announce the launch of exciting new STEAM programs at Ulloa and Sunset Elementary Schools.

This development underscores our dedication to tailoring our educational initiatives to meet the specific needs of each community. We believe that by introducing these STEAM programs, we can contribute significantly to the academic growth and development of students at Ulloa and Sunset Elementary Schools, providing them with enriching and innovative learning experiences.

Our commitment extends beyond mere program implementation, aiming to make a positive impact on the unique educational landscape of each school, fostering a supportive and engaging environment for students to thrive.

## STEAMHEAD PRODUCTIONS

We launched SteamHead Productions with the aim of capturing and disseminating the stories of our students, alongside sharing our accumulated knowledge with educators worldwide.

Collaborating with the non-profit organization Cultivative and our media director Mike Shaw, we transformed this initiative into a comprehensive platform. Our goal was to showcase our students in a professional manner and to spark inspiration among a broad audience.

## MakeFashion Edu Online Course

Empowering students to design their future, one stitch at a time.

Our MakeFashion Edu Online Course brings the intersection of technology and self-expression into classrooms worldwide. Through a series of engaging modules, students learn design thinking, electronics, and fashion tech, transforming ideas into wearable creations. This course isn’t just about fashion — it’s about giving students the tools to tell their stories and build their confidence through innovation.

## MAKERSPACE BADGES

Building skills, earning achievements, and creating futures.

The Makerspace Badges give teachers a pathway to guide students through meaningful makerspace experiences. Not "how to 3D print" but why and for who, basing learning on empathy and authentic problem solving. Each badge represents a step forward in mastery — whether it’s soldering circuits, 3D printing, interviewing clients, or sketching ideas.

## SHTEAM Labs Activites

Documenting our journey, one lesson at a time.

The SHTEAM Labs Activities Journals are our latest collection of lesson plans, student reflections, and activity guides. They are custom tailored resources designed for particular classrooms, and you might find that they suit your classrooms as well!
