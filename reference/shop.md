## ONLINE COURSE

The MakeFashion Edu Online Course integrates technology and creativity into classrooms across the globe. With engaging modules, students explore design thinking, electronics, and fashion tech, turning their ideas into wearable creations. More than just fashion, this course equips students with the skills to express themselves and boost their confidence through innovation.
