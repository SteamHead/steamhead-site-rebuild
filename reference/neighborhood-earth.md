# Neighborhood Earth

Neighborhood Earth represents our outreach efforts and community activism all around the world.

It is our longest running program, existing before SteamHead. In 2011 James entered China as a manufacturing engineer, found that locals highly valued education, and started free classes for factory workers. Since then, James and Carrie have held free classes in Brazil, Germany, Canada, China, the U.K., and the U.S.

## VIRTUAL SPACE

The Virtual Maker Exhibition marks SteamHead’s inaugural multi-school online event!

This platform allows teachers and students to present their projects in a dynamic online environment that combines the elements of a video game with live conferencing features.

In early 2020, amidst the ongoing pandemic, we seized the chance to explore innovative approaches to exhibition and collaboration. This led us to discover new methods and mediums for interacting in familiar ways. What we create now paves the way for the future of work and education!

## MAKERSPACE

Now retired, the SteamHead Makerspace in Shenzhen became a cornerstone of creativity and collaboration.

Far more than just a collection of tools and gadgets, it was a lively community hub where locals and expatriates came together to spark innovation, unleash their creativity, and forge powerful partnerships. Founded in 2017 by educators Carrie and James, this makerspace rapidly emerged as a beacon of hands-on learning in the heart of one of the world’s most dynamic engineering cities, fostering a culture of curiosity and making ideas come to life.

## SteamHead R.ED

The SteamHead Resident Educators program was at the heart of our vibrant community, embodying our commitment to fostering creativity and collaboration. We proudly welcomed educators from around the world, inviting both local and international guests to immerse themselves in our innovative makerspace, particularly in our dedicated Resident Room. Although our popular Shenzhen location is currently suspended, the program elevated Maker Embedded learning through enriching cultural exchanges and diverse perspectives.

Participants engaged in hands-on workshops, collaborative projects, and cultural tours, fostering unique experiences and lasting relationships with our residents and the city's educational institutions. Those who joined us at SteamHead became part of a dynamic network, advancing creativity and collaboration while promoting global educational excellence.

## Maker Events

Bringing communities together, one journey at a time.

Our Maker Events are at the heart of SteamHead’s mission to unite people through shared creativity. From local hackathons to international maker fairs, we bring students, educators, and innovators into hands-on spaces where ideas come to life. These events aren’t just about making—they’re about connecting across borders and sparking the next wave of community-driven innovation.
